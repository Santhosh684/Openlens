## **OpenLens – Unified AI Web Analyzer**

**Overview**
OpenLens is a Streamlit-based AI application that integrates **real-time web content fetching, intelligent article summarization, and interactive Q\&A** in one unified interface.
It allows users to either:

1. **Summarize & query any online article** using advanced AI models (LLaMA 3.1 via the Together API).
2. **Explore fresh online data** from trusted sources like news portals, stock market blogs, and Reddit, then instantly analyze linked articles.

---

**Key Features**

* **Automated Article Extraction:** Cleans and extracts relevant text from any given URL using `BeautifulSoup`.
* **AI-Powered Summaries & Q\&A:** Uses LLaMA 3.1 to generate concise summaries and answer context-specific questions without adding speculation.
* **Real-Time Data Fetching:** Fetches trending news, finance blogs, and social posts in one click.
* **Two Modes of Use:**

  * *URL Summarizer* – Summarize and analyze articles on demand.
  * *Web Data Explorer* – Browse live data sources and auto-analyze selected items.
* **Session Memory:** Stores your recent analyses for quick access and comparison.
* **Seamless UX:** Query parameters allow clicking a link in Explorer mode to auto-trigger summarization.

---

**Why It’s Needed**

* **Information Overload:** News and online content flood in constantly, making it hard to grasp the core ideas quickly.
* **Scattered Data Sources:** Users often have to jump between multiple tabs for news, blogs, and forums; OpenLens centralizes them.
* **Faster Research:** Ideal for analysts, journalists, or researchers who need concise, accurate digests without reading full articles.
* **Engaging AI Interaction:** The assistant answers questions based only on provided content—ensuring focused, context-relevant responses.
* **Custom Tone:** A light sarcastic touch in summaries keeps reading engaging without losing professionalism.

---

**Pros**

* Saves time by **compressing long content into actionable insights**.
* Supports **both proactive browsing and reactive URL analysis**.
* Maintains **private session-based memory** without storing external data.
* Easy to use and **deployable on Streamlit Cloud**.
* Modular design allows easy integration of **more data sources or AI models** in the future.
